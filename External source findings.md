# External source findings

## Source [1]: Geometric Unity official site
URL: https://geometricunity.org/

The official site describes Geometric Unity as Eric Weinstein’s attempt to recover apparently incompatible geometries of fundamental physics from a general structure with minimal assumptions. It links the 2013 Oxford lecture and a 2021 draft manuscript, and frames the project as a unified-field proposal. This supports the historical identity and stated ambition of GU, but does not by itself establish the theory’s equations, empirical validation, or acceptance.

## Source [2]: Peter Woit, “Eric Weinstein on Geometric Unity”
URL: https://www.math.columbia.edu/~woit/wordpress/?p=5927

Woit’s 2013 commentary records the public framing around the relationship between Standard Model gauge geometry and four-dimensional pseudo-Riemannian spacetime. In a later comment, Woit explains the elementary dimension count: a metric tensor in four dimensions is a symmetric bilinear form with 10 components, so one can form a bundle over a 4D base with 10D fibers. The resulting 14D total-space count is a mathematical construction, not evidence that GU derives the Standard Model. The page also records skepticism about the absence of detailed published technical exposition at that time.

## Source [3]: Choudhury, “Riemannian Metric Bundle,” arXiv:2304.07116
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07116

The abstract defines a Riemannian metric bundle as a fiber bundle over a smooth manifold whose fibers are spaces of symmetric positive-definite bilinear forms on tangent spaces. This supports the general mathematical meaning of a metric bundle. It is not a source validating the Sovereign Engine’s use of GU or any physics claim.

## Source [4]: nLab, “fiber bundles in physics”
URL: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/fiber+bundles+in+physics

The page provides a conceptual explanation of bundles as global structures that locally resemble products but may carry nontrivial transition data. It explains why gauge fields, spin structures, characteristic classes, instantons, and geometric quantization are naturally expressed with bundles and connections. It is useful background for making the project’s bundle language digestible, but it is a secondary reference and should not be treated as a project-specific proof.

## Evidence policy

Claims in the final dossier will be labeled as observed repository behavior, mathematical definition/identity, project hypothesis, tested software contract, or unresolved/unsupported assertion. The repository’s own claims will not be upgraded to mathematical or physical fact solely because they appear in README files or tests.
