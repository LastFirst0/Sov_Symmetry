# Geometric-Language Research Findings

**Collection date:** 2026-08-18  
**Use:** Background for a falsifiable research program; not evidence that Sovereign Engine has discovered a universal geometry of language.

| Source | Observed finding | Program implication |
|---|---|---|
| He et al., *Language Models as Hierarchy Encoders*, NeurIPS 2024 | The paper retrains encoder language models in hyperbolic space and reports better task results for transitive inference, subsumption prediction, and transfer across hierarchies than its stated baselines. | Test hyperbolic geometry only on preregistered hierarchy tasks against Euclidean and task-matched baselines; do not generalize a hierarchy result to all language. |
| Yavas et al., *On the Relation Between Fine-Tuning, Topological Properties, and Task Performance in Sense-Enhanced Embeddings*, ACL 2025 | The reported relationship between isotropy, uniformity, sense-alignment, and performance varies by learning method and task. | Measure geometry, linguistic behavior, and task performance separately; require held-out replication and report cases where geometric metrics do not predict performance. |
| Pedashenko et al., *Unveiling Intrinsic Dimension of Texts*, EACL 2026 | The paper studies intrinsic dimension in contemporary model representations and reports genre-linked variation in its experimental setting. | Intrinsic dimension is a measurable candidate statistic, not a discovered ontological dimension. Controls must include text length, genre, model, layer, randomization, and corpus split. |

## Research constraint

The defensible claim is: **a specified representation, on a specified corpus and task, has measured geometric statistics under a specified estimator.** A stronger claim that the same statistic identifies the true higher-dimensional geometry of language, mind, or reality requires substantially different theoretical and empirical support.
