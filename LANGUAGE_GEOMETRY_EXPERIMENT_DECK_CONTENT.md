# Presentation Content: From Geometry Ambition to Testable Evidence

## Cover

**From Geometry Ambition to Testable Evidence**  
**Deferred mathematics, a fail-closed feedback loop, and the first language-hierarchy experiment**

## Slide 1

### Two systems now have distinct jobs

- The legacy runtime is an **experimental proposal engine** for geometric transforms, representations, and hypotheses.
- The universal kernel is an **evidence gate** for exact contracts, artifacts, replay, and bounded outcomes.
- Their complementarity comes from separation: **proposal is not proof; a receipt is not a theory verdict.**

**Visual:** Split field—“Explore” on the left, “Verify” on the right—joined by a narrow controlled evidence channel.

## Slide 2

### The trusted core deliberately became smaller

| Former geometry-native surface | Current disposition |
|---|---|
| Riemann sphere, Möbius transforms, Fubini–Study distance | Deferred as numerical/computational adapter candidates |
| Hopf map, torus updates, invariant probes | Deferred pending precise inputs, tolerances, fixtures, and non-claims |
| E8 snapping, C60/E8 slots, corpus quantization | Research candidates—not verifier semantics |
| Broad coherence/runtime labels | Removed from trusted verdict language |

**Takeaway:** The mathematics was not declared false; it lost **automatic authority**.

## Slide 3

### Geometry can return—one bounded claim at a time

- A future adapter must declare its object, predicate, assumptions, reference behavior, fixtures, and explicit non-claims.
- The framework label cannot select code or cause a passing result.
- A successful finite check may support a narrow mathematical statement; it cannot validate E8, language, cognition, physics, or reality.

**Visual:** Admission gate: “defined object → named relation → fixtures → independent review → bounded receipt.”

## Slide 4

### The loop separates proposing from proving

1. A human freezes a hypothesis, dataset, split, baseline, metric, budget, and falsification rule.
2. The legacy runtime produces a candidate artifact in quarantine.
3. An independent evaluator recomputes the benchmark from pinned inputs.
4. The universal kernel verifies manifests, hashes, allowed predicates, and gate conditions.
5. A human review authority decides whether the result stays quarantined or becomes an admitted experimental adapter.

**Visual:** Circular five-stage evidence loop with an explicit human decision checkpoint—no autonomous promotion arrow.

## Slide 5

### No component gets to certify itself

| Control | Why it exists |
|---|---|
| Frozen proposal digest | No changing the task after seeing results |
| Independent evaluator | Candidate metrics must be recomputed |
| Baseline + ablation suite | A geometry effect must beat simpler explanations |
| Holdout + replication | One favorable run is not a discovery |
| Append-only evidence | Results cannot be silently rewritten |
| Human release authority | Values and interpretation remain accountable |

**Takeaway:** The loop learns from failure; it does not reward self-confirmation.

## Slide 6

### First pilot used a public lexical hierarchy

- **Corpus:** Open English WordNet 2025, CC-BY 4.0; a public graph of English lexical relations.
- **Task:** Recover the direct parent of an unseen node in a finite sampled hypernym hierarchy rooted at “animal.”
- **Inputs:** Text-only TF-IDF vectors made from synset lemmas and definitions; hierarchy links were used only as evaluation labels.
- **Comparison:** Euclidean cosine baseline versus a declared Poincaré-ball candidate with text-derived radial coordinates.

**Source:** Open English WordNet and the reproducible local sample/manifest.

## Slide 7

### The hyperbolic candidate did not win this pilot

| Held-out metric | Euclidean cosine | Poincaré candidate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Top-1 parent recovery | **9.1%** | 0.0% |
| Top-3 parent recovery | **36.4%** | 18.2% |
| Mean reciprocal rank | **0.273** | 0.194 |
| Mean rank | 10.727 | 10.727 |

- The 95% bootstrap interval for candidate minus baseline Top-1 recovery was **−27.3 to 0.0 percentage points**.
- Verdict: **no supported hierarchy advantage on this sample.**

**Source:** `hierarchy_recovery_v0` reproducible experiment artifacts.

## Slide 8

### A negative result is evidence, not a setback to hide

- The candidate beat the random reference but lost to the simple text-only Euclidean baseline.
- This rules out one tempting claim: the tested heuristic does **not** show an advantage for language-hierarchy recovery on this sample.
- It does not rule out all hyperbolic methods, all geometry, or a future better-specified legacy transform.
- It explicitly does not test E8, Hopf fibrations, cognition, or a universal geometry of language.

**Visual:** Narrow result box surrounded by a larger “not concluded” field.

## Slide 9

### The next question is precise, not mystical

| Hypothesis | Honest test |
|---|---|
| Hyperbolic hierarchy benefit | Beat matched Euclidean baselines on preregistered held-out tasks |
| Intrinsic-dimension signal | Survive controls for length, genre, layer, corpus, and estimator |
| Topology predicts capability | Outperform standard metrics on held-out prediction |
| Legacy geometry adds value | Beat equally expressive non-E8 transforms under matched budgets |

**Rule:** A result may win, fail, or remain unresolved. Every outcome is informative.

## Slide 10

### The path forward is controlled exploration

1. Freeze experiment schemas, manifests, and tamper fixtures.
2. Build a quarantined evaluator with pinned public data and no release-key access.
3. Compare candidate, Euclidean, random, and matched non-E8 controls.
4. Publish results and limits—including negative cases.
5. Admit only independently reproduced, scoped experimental adapters.

**Closing statement:** Preserve ambition. Demand evidence. Keep the meaning of each result honest.
