# Sovereign Engine Work-Package Closeout v0.1

**Prepared by:** Manus AI  
**Closeout date:** 2026-08-18  
**Scope:** Theory-agnostic structural verification kernel, governed research evidence, read-only control dashboard, and bounded operating policy.

## Closeout statement

The completed work establishes a small offline deterministic kernel that evaluates declared finite structural predicates and returns only **`verified`**, **`fail`**, or **`unverifiable`**. It does not identify the ultimate truth of a theory, infer physical mechanism, interpret lived experience, or substitute a statistical result for a scientific or personal conclusion. The surrounding platform now preserves source provenance, experimental limits, negative evidence, release-attestation boundaries, and operator-facing research records as explicit artifacts.

> The current artifact set is **evidence-bounded, not self-authorizing**. A dashboard, source connector, external skill, or model output cannot promote an experiment, adapter, release, or claim on its own.

## Explicit remaining decisions

The following are not implementation defects; they are deliberate human gates. A production signing key and custody policy must be approved before a signed bundle can move from `unverifiable` to a validated attestation state. A future molecular experiment must begin with a new frozen work package and protocol; M0 does not justify reusing its data, method, or language to make biological, medical, clinical, therapeutic, disease, longevity, tissue, or personal-genomic claims. The candidate external adapter requires its eight-gate evidence package before admission.

No connector was enabled or modified, no schedule was created, no external skill was imported, and no automatic deployment or publication action was taken during this closeout.
