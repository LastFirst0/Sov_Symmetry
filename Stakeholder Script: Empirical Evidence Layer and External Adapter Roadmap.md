# Stakeholder Script: Empirical Evidence Layer and External Adapter Roadmap

## Intended duration: 10–12 minutes

### Opening

“Today we are extending the finished structural kernel without changing its core promise. The kernel remains theory-agnostic and bounded. The next architecture adds two things: a way to describe real-world evidence responsibly, and a way for independent research communities to contribute adapters fairly.”

### Why empirical evidence is separate

“A matrix or graph check can tell us whether a stated finite structure satisfies a named rule. It cannot tell us whether a measurement is reliable, a sample is representative, a model fits nature, or a theory is true. Those require data provenance, uncertainty, a declared method, and review. The empirical packet makes that missing process visible instead of hiding it.”

### Empirical packet

“Every packet identifies a target quantity, data versions and access conditions, transformations, responsible people or organizations, uncertainty components, and a separately versioned analysis binding. The structural kernel validates that the evidence description is complete. It returns ‘cannot be concluded by the structural kernel alone’ until an external analysis receipt is attached.”

### Uncertainty

“An estimate is not enough. We preserve the uncertainty components, how they were evaluated, how they were combined, any coverage factor, and the basis for any confidence statement. If a basis is missing, the packet records that absence instead of silently inventing meaning.”

### External adapter admission

“The first external adapter is not a partnership announcement and not an endorsement of a framework. It is a governance test. A candidate must pass eight gates: intent, semantics, fixtures, pure replay, provenance-label neutrality, assurance, independent review, and immutable release.”

### The important non-claim

“Admission means only this: a particular adapter version can deterministically evaluate one declared predicate over one declared finite object. It does not mean that the source theory is true, complete, preferred, or scientifically confirmed.”

### Closing

“The common rule across both tracks is simple: identity, assumptions, methods, evidence, scope, replay, and non-claims must be visible before a result can influence a receipt or a public statement. That is how Sovereign Engine makes evidence more portable without making it automatically authoritative.”
