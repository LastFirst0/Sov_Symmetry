# Sovereign Engine Dashboard — Design Exploration

## Three candidate directions

### 1. Mission Control Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A deep-space operations board where roadmap execution feels like a measured flight plan: dark mineral surfaces, red telemetry signals, and disciplined technical typography. The interface makes evidence, gates, and dependencies feel consequential rather than decorative.
**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Research Folio
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm editorial archive that treats the roadmap as a living research dossier, mixing oversized serif headings with precise annotation tables. It would feel scholarly, calm, and documentation-first.
**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Geometric Instrument Panel
**Very Brief Intro:** A bright technical laboratory interface built around graph-paper grids, modular readouts, and geometric field plots. It foregrounds mathematical exploration and rapid experiment navigation.
**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen direction — Mission Control Ledger

### Design Movement
The dashboard follows **industrial research operations**, combining space-program mission-control discipline with the restrained typography and redline annotation language of advanced engineering notebooks.

### Core Principles
1. Every visual element must answer an operational question: what is active, what is verified, what is blocked, and what comes next.
2. The interface relies on layered spatial hierarchy rather than rounded-card repetition: large panel fields, fine rules, and deliberate red telemetry markers.
3. Mathematical structure appears as a quiet background language—coordinate grids, orbit lines, and invariant markers—never as visual noise.
4. Status is semantic: colors, symbols, and labels always pair to avoid ambiguity.

### Color Philosophy
Near-black graphite and mineral blue establish a serious command surface; oxidized red is the signature intervention color for gates, alerts, and evidence markers; soft ivory creates a high-contrast reading field for narrative panels. The palette should communicate control, accountability, and research gravity rather than generic sci-fi spectacle.

### Layout Paradigm
Use a persistent narrow command rail, a spacious asymmetric main canvas, and a contextual intelligence column. The default desktop composition should feel like a program war room: the top insight field sets direction, the center presents milestone and workstream systems, and a right-side evidence field surfaces the most consequential operating details.

### Signature Elements
1. Fine red coordinate ticks and crosshair markers at key section boundaries.
2. A “signal line” that threads through phases and milestone gates as the primary roadmap visual.
3. Ledger-style status stamps such as VERIFIED, ACTIVE, and UNRESOLVED paired with small square indicators.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions reveal context rather than merely decorate. Selecting a workstream focuses its artifacts, dependencies, and next action. Filters narrow the command surface without hiding the program’s global state. Motion should be restrained and functional: panel focus, filter transitions, and signal-line emphasis.

### Animation
Use 160–220ms opacity and transform transitions with a crisp ease-out. The signal line may gently draw in once on first load, with reduced-motion support disabling it. Hover states should sharpen panel contrast and move a red marker by a few pixels; controls should respond with a 0.97 active scale.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for strategic headlines and **IBM Plex Mono** for statuses, equations, labels, and metadata. Headlines are editorial and commanding; operational content is monospaced, compact, and line-height conscious. No Inter.

### Brand Essence
**Sovereign Engine is a verifiable meaning operations platform for teams building geometry-native intelligence without obscuring evidence.** Personality: disciplined, investigative, sovereign.

### Brand Voice
Headlines should be declarative and operational; CTAs should name the next decision; microcopy should identify evidence and limitations plainly. Examples: “Advance only when the invariant survives.” and “This claim is unresolved, not ignored.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use an abstract four-point coordinate lockup: a red square signal at the center of two offset graphite axes, implying base, fiber, and controlled transformation. The wordmark is composed through an engineered serif/mono pairing rather than a default font treatment.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Oxide — #D64635**. A vivid but grounded red that signifies a decision point, evidence event, or active gate.
