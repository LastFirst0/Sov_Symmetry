External protocol findings: DSSE v1.0.2 defines signatures over PAE(UTF8(PAYLOAD_TYPE), SERIALIZED_BODY), requires application-specific payload types rather than generic application/json, treats KEYID as an unauthenticated hint, and warns that the verified serialized body must be the same body delivered to the application. Source: https://github.com/secure-systems-lab/dsse/blob/master/protocol.md (reviewed 2026-08-17). RFC 9162 describes Merkle-tree inclusion proofs and consistency proofs for append-only logs and notes that conflicting roots or log behavior can expose misbehavior. Source: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc9162 (reviewed 2026-08-17).
